I — Preface —
A NOTE FROM THE COUNTER

A shop that refuses to let a good phone die.

Every device that crosses our counter arrives with a small emergency attached. A screen meets a floor. A monsoon finds a pocket. A motherboard, without warning, decides it is finished. Our trade is the stubborn conviction that none of these accidents is final — that with a bench, a microscope, and an honest price, most of what the market calls dead is merely interrupted.

We keep the good panels. We fit genuine cells, matched capacities, and we refuse — politely, permanently — to build a repair on a part we would not put in our own phones. If a component is not good enough to survive our own pockets, it does not survive our counter. In a country where a phone is often the most expensive machine a family owns, this is not romance; it is arithmetic. It has cost us margin. It has never cost us a customer twice.

We believe in the right to fix. A phone you own should not expire on a software roadmap, and a repair should not cost more than the truth. So we quote before we open a screw, we return every removed part in a small bag, and we tell you plainly when a repair is not worth making. Verdicts are free. Noise is extra.

What follows is not a brochure. It is a record of the work, set the way the trade deserves — slowly, and in gold.

01
Screen & Display Glass · OLED · Assemblies · Walk-in

The glass breaks. The day doesn't have to.

Displays replaced while you take tea — glass, OLED and full assemblies, fitted, laminated and pressure-tested before they leave the bench.

Service Screens, glass & display assemblies
Turnaround 45–90 minutes, walk-in
Warranty 6 months, parts & fitting
Coverage iPhone · Samsung · Xiaomi · Realme · Tecno · Symphony

A screen is the only part of the phone you ever actually touch. It deserves better than the cheapest flat-panel on the market, glued in haste under a ceiling fan. We fit original and OEM-grade assemblies, we check touch response corner to corner, and we do not hand a phone back until the colours sit exactly where the factory left them.

And when the green line appears on a Samsung AMOLED after an update — the defect the whole country has been arguing about — we test whether the maker's known-defect replacement still covers your set before you spend a single taka. Half the panels we refuse to sell were never the customer's to buy.

Crushed glass, ghost touch, ink blots, black corners — the display has a whole vocabulary of failure, and we speak all of it. Where only the glass is broken on a unit that still has a healthy panel, we separate and refit rather than replace the whole assembly, because your data and your money both matter more than our margin.1

A repair is a promise the shop keeps finishing.

02
Motherboard & Chip-Level The Heirloom Chapter · Micro-Soldering

Where other shops see a dead board, we see unasked questions.

Microscope work, reballing, trace repair — the slow, patient discipline of bringing logic boards back from verdicts handed down too quickly.

Service Motherboard & chip-level repair
Turnaround 2–5 days, case by case
Warranty 3 months on the repaired fault
Coverage Dead sets, no display, no charge, water boards

This is the heirloom chapter. Most phones are not dead — they are one component away from living, and the component costs less than lunch. The difference between a verdict and a repair is a microscope, a steady hand, and the willingness to test rather than guess. We work at 10–45× magnification with hot air and micro-irons, reballing ICs, rebuilding pads, and running jumper wires thinner than an argument.2

Boards are washed, corrosion is mapped, and every repair is photographed under the scope before and after, so you can see precisely what was wrong and what we did about it. No shortcuts, no "board-level problem, buy a new one" — unless the board truly says so.

Dead is a rumour. We interrogate the witnesses.

2 Our scope has seen more drama than most television.

03
Battery & Power Genuine Cells · Safety First

A swollen battery is not a feature. It is a warning.

Genuine cells fitted in thirty minutes — with the old cell returned to you in a bag, because proof is part of the service.

Service Batteries, charging ports & power
Turnaround 30 minutes, most models
Warranty 12 months on genuine cells
Coverage iPhone · Samsung · Xiaomi · Tecno · tablets · power banks

We fit genuine and grade-A cells only, and we will happily show you the difference under the counter light: matched capacity, honest cycle counts, thermal protection that actually exists. An aftermarket cell is a small fire with a discount sticker on it. We do not stock them. When another counter offers you an "original" iPhone battery for the price of lunch, ask what is actually inside the wrapper — the genuine cell costs real money because it is real. We have, in ten years, never once used a cheap cable.3

Charging trouble is usually not the battery at all — it is the port, the IC beneath it, or the cable that came free with a phone case. We diagnose the whole chain before we sell you anything, and we test every replacement to full charge before it goes home in your pocket.

The old cell comes back to you in a bag. Proof is part of the service.

Software repair is surgery without anaesthetic: the patient watches everything. So we explain each step before we take it, we back up first — always first — and we never touch data we have not been asked to touch.4 The small faults cost a cup of tea's worth of taka; the large ones cost an afternoon. Both are quoted before a single setting changes.

FRP and lock work is done for rightful owners with proof of ownership — bring the box, the receipt, or the account itself. The machines are capable of almost anything; the shop, of rather less. That is deliberate.

The data matters more than the device. The device, we can always fix.

Whatever the liquid — pond, tea, chlorine, or the sea — the procedure is the same: open, wash, map the corrosion, dry with care, and then test honestly before quoting a single taka. The brand counters charge ৳500 merely to hear the complaint; here, the listening is free. The diagnosis costs nothing, and it comes with a straight answer.5

Ultrasonic baths lift what the flood leaves behind; under the scope we rebuild what the bath uncovers. Some boards are beyond saving, and we will tell you so for free — because a verdict you can trust is worth more than a repair you cannot.

Free diagnosis. Honest verdicts. Repairs only if the board deserves them.
